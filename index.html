Scheduling BD Agent

Scheduling BD Agent is a private business-development assistant used by Omar Sobeih, a construction scheduling and project-controls consultant. It is not a public service and has no other users.

It helps Omar find construction contractors from public procurement records (bid results, plan holders and contract awards), keep his own client records, and send his outreach emails from his own mailbox. When a prospect agrees to a meeting, it uses Omar's own Google account to:

  • Google Calendar - check when Omar is free, and create the meeting (with a Google Meet link) and its invitation.
  • Google Drive - keep backup copies of the assistant's own database in a folder it creates (it can only see files it created).

Read the privacy policy for how this data is handled.
