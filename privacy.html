Privacy Policy

Omar The Scheduler - last updated 28 September 2026

Omar The Scheduler ("the app") is a private assistant operated by Omar Sobeih for his own construction scheduling and project-controls consulting business. The only Google account it connects to is Omar's own. Contact: omarthescheduler@gmail.com.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (read free/busy and events; create events): to find times when Omar is available and to create a meeting, with a Google Meet link and an invitation, when a prospect asks to meet.
  • Google Drive (only files the app creates): to store backup copies of the app's own database in a folder it creates, and to delete its older backups. The app cannot see any other Drive files.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features above to the account owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with or transferred to anyone else, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models. No person other than the account owner reads it.

Omar The Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Google access tokens are stored encrypted in its local database. From the calendar it keeps only what a booked meeting needs (time, Meet link and the attendee's email address) in the owner's client records.

Other information

The app keeps business contact information about construction companies gathered from public sources (such as public procurement records and company websites) for the owner's outreach. Anyone contacted can reply to ask not to be contacted again, and that request is honoured permanently.

Revoking access and deletion

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for information about you to be deleted, email omarthescheduler@gmail.com.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
